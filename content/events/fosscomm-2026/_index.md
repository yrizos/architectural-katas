[FOSSCOMM](https://2026.fosscomm.gr/) is the annual summit of the Greek free and open-source community. It has run since 2008, is free to attend, and is organized entirely by volunteers, with a focus on digital freedom, privacy, and open technology.

The 2026 edition was held on 31 October and 1 November at the University of West Attica in Athens. Its audience mixes seasoned professionals, academics, and newcomers, and the program spans talks, workshops, and community booths.

This was the 19th edition, and the organizers expected more than 400 attendees, over 50 presentations, and more than 20 communities. Booths ranged from Tor, KDE, and GNOME to Lichess, the Godot game engine, and the Hellenic IT Museum.
