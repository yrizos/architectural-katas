[Open Conf](https://open-conf.gr/) is an annual tech conference in Athens, backed by the Greek software and IT sector, from major companies to open communities, startups, hubs, and research centers. It draws more than 1,000 attendees a year, and the 2026 edition runs on 20 and 21 November at the Dais Events Center in Marousi.

This year's themes are AI-Native Engineering, Tech with Impact, and Software Craftsmanship, with cybersecurity and leadership running across all three. The program combines talks, workshops, open space sessions, and 1-to-1 mentoring.

Since its first edition in May 2021, Open Conf has run 9 conferences and 5 hackathons with the help of more than 50 volunteers. Its stated mission is to make Greece a destination for companies, hubs, universities, and VCs looking for partners in the global tech ecosystem.
