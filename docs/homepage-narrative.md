# Homepage Narrative

The homepage takes the reader through a guided attempt at a kata.

## Understand the Exercise

Explain what a kata is, what the reader will do, and what they need to begin.

## Encounter the Problem

Introduce the conference brief as the shared problem the reader will work through.

The transition into the brief connects the explanation of the exercise to the problem the reader will tackle. It needs to establish that connection rather than repeat instructions about designing a system.

## Investigate the Problem

Identify priorities, missing information, and assumptions before drawing.

## Develop a Proposal

Compare alternatives, choose an approach, and explain its costs.

## Challenge the Reasoning

Change a requirement or invite questions, then reconsider the decisions.
