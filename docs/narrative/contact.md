# Contact

## Purpose

This page says who introduced architectural katas at Epignosis and how to reach them.

The reader leaves knowing who to talk to and why these two people run the katas.

## Key Information

- Aggelos Bellos and Yannis Rizos introduced architectural katas at Epignosis.
- Why. Most developers design a system from scratch only a few times in their career, and real projects leave them little room to build that judgment. Epignosis runs katas so everyone on its teams gets that practice, away from production. The source is the current About page.
- The goal is what Gregor Hohpe calls architecture without architects. The teams own their architecture, and when a major decision comes up, anyone on the team can act as the architect for a while. The source is the current About page.
- People.
  - Aggelos Bellos, Staff Engineer at Epignosis. GitHub `bellangelo`, LinkedIn `in/aggelos-bellos`.
  - Yannis Rizos, Director of Engineering at Epignosis. GitHub `yrizos`, LinkedIn `in/yrizos`.
- Contact details are GitHub and LinkedIn only. The page has no email address and no contact form.
- The people cards use the existing `people` and `person` shortcodes.
