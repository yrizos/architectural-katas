The Show Must Go On!

Description

A team of organizers runs a large annual technology conference, bringing together thousands of developers, technology professionals, and industry experts.

Each year features a new lineup of speakers, talks, and activities. The organizers need a platform to manage the conference, keep attendees informed, and support interactions between speakers and participants.

Early-bird ticket sales open in three months.

The platform must also maintain an archive of previous editions, allowing visitors to explore past schedules, speakers, and presentations.
