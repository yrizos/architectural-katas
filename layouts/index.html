Practice System Design

An architectural kata gives you a brief for a fictional system and asks you to work out a design. The brief leaves questions unanswered, so part of the work is deciding what you need to know and which assumptions you can proceed with.

You choose what matters most, compare alternatives, and sketch a proposal. Each important choice comes with a cost you need to explain. Someone else may reach a different design from the same brief, and comparing your reasoning can expose priorities or assumptions worth reconsidering.

You can work alone or in a team of three to five. Set aside an hour or two with paper or a whiteboard. You won't write code. Start with the conference brief below, then use the practice steps to question and revise your proposal.

Design a Conference Platform

For this kata, you'll design a platform for a fictional technology conference. The organizers need to manage the event and keep attendees informed when the venue's network is unreliable.

Read their request and note what you need to clarify before choosing an approach.

The Show Must Go On!

Description

A team of organizers runs a large annual technology conference, bringing together thousands of developers, technology professionals, and industry experts.

Each year features a new lineup of speakers, talks, and activities. The organizers need a platform to manage the conference, keep attendees informed, and support interactions between speakers and participants.

Early-bird ticket sales open on December 1, 2026.

The platform must also maintain an archive of previous editions, allowing visitors to explore past schedules, speakers, and presentations.
