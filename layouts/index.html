Practice System Design

An architectural kata gives you a brief for a fictional system and asks you to work out a design. The brief leaves questions unanswered, so part of the work is deciding what you need to know and which assumptions you can proceed with.

You choose what matters most, compare alternatives, and sketch a proposal. Each important choice comes with a cost you need to explain. Someone else may reach a different design from the same brief, and comparing your reasoning can expose priorities or assumptions worth reconsidering.

You can work alone or in a team of three to five. Set aside an hour or two with paper or a whiteboard. You won't write code. Start with the conference brief below, then use the practice steps to question and revise your proposal.
